Deep inspiration breath hold

Breath Hold Coach

Practise the deep breath hold used in breast radiotherapy. Your phone rests on your chest, measures how high your breath lifts it, and tells you by sound whether you are holding above the line.

Before you start

  1. Turn the ringer on (silent switch off) and turn the volume up.
  2. Lie on your back, arms where your treatment team positions them.
  3. Place the phone screen up, flat on the lower part of your breastbone, top edge towards your chin.
  4. Breathe into your chest, not your belly, unless your team told you otherwise.

The app first checks the signal, then asks for one minute of calm breathing and three deep breaths to learn your range. Practice starts on its own after that.

Session

    Feedback

    Each phase repeats its hold and rest the set number of times, then the next phase starts. Use phases to build up, for example 2 × 15 s, then 2 × 20 s. Most treatment holds are 20 to 25 seconds.
