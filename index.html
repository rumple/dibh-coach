    PhaseHoldsHoldRest

      Most treatment holds are 20 to 25 seconds.

      Get ready

      1. Turn the ringer on and the volume up.
      2. Lie on your back, arms where your treatment team positions them.
      3. Place the phone screen up, flat on the lower part of your breastbone, top edge towards your chin.
      4. Breathe into your chest, not your belly, unless your team told you otherwise.

      First a signal check, then about a minute of calm and deep breaths to learn your range. Practice starts on its own after that.

      Practice only. This app does not connect to or match any treatment machine, and its percentages are not your clinical threshold. Stop and breathe normally if you feel dizzy or short of breath. Check with your treatment team before practising if you have heart or lung problems.
